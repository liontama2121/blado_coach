# Landing — Blado

Scope: public landing (`/`). Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: adults in Bogotá who want to lose fat, build muscle or improve their health, often training at home. Action: book an assessment or message on WhatsApp. Proof: data-driven follow-up (measurements, body-fat %, monthly photos) shown with clearly labelled sample data. No invented testimonials, prices or certifications.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Negro Lima. Premium training energy that comes from restraint: a graphite ground, giant condensed caps, and fluorescent lime spent only where action and the key word live. It evolves the chosen Cartel Fluorescente (poster bands, the line-up shouted like an event) at premium scale. It refuses the category default: a bodybuilder photo, neon glow everywhere, three service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Graphite #0D0E0D ground, off-white #EDEFEA text, lime #C8FF1A for the CTA, one italic key word and the moving bottom band, and grey #5B615C as secondary. Sofia Sans Extra Condensed 900 for display (uppercase, italic for emphasis); Geist for body text; Geist Mono for small data labels. Radius 0, hairline borders (#232623), no glow. Raises kept: overprint multiply in the photo comparator; fixed digit slots that roll on view; named metric states (bajando / estable / subiendo / sin dato); a circular goal stamp.

STORY: The visitor learns that Blado trains them at home or in the gym, with follow-up online. They come to believe progress is measured monthly and shown. Then they book an assessment.
